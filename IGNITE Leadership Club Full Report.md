# IGNITE Leadership Club

## Strategic Report, Pitch Update, and Implementation Guide

## 1. Executive Summary

IGNITE Leadership Club is a school-based youth development and orientation programme designed to prepare secondary school students for life beyond examinations. The programme responds to a major gap in the current education system: students are often trained to pass exams, but not intentionally prepared for leadership, personal growth, civic responsibility, social responsibility, career direction, and practical community impact.

IGNITE is not designed as a one-time seminar or motivational event. It is designed as a sustainable youth development system embedded within secondary schools. Through regular training, mentorship, student leadership structures, school liaison officers, teacher/counsellor support, guest speakers, and monitoring and evaluation, IGNITE aims to build lasting behavioural and developmental outcomes among young people.

The proposed pilot will run for one school term across two secondary schools in Zamfara State, engaging 60 students from JSS3 to SS3. The pilot will focus on improved student behaviour, leadership skills, career clarity, and community service preparation. The long-term vision is to scale the programme across Zamfara State and then into other parts of Northwest Nigeria through government adoption, NGO support, CSR partnerships, and private sponsorship.

## 2. Background and Problem Statement

Many Nigerian youths pass through school without receiving structured preparation for real-life responsibility. The formal school system largely focuses on academic performance and examination success, while issues such as leadership, values, discipline, civic duty, career direction, emotional maturity, social responsibility, and personal development are often left unaddressed.

This gap is especially important in a society where young people face increasing pressure from unemployment, social instability, insecurity, peer influence, weak mentorship systems, and limited exposure to positive role models. When students are not properly guided, the same society that contributed to the problem often becomes the environment they return to after school.

Short-term seminars and campaigns can create temporary excitement, but they rarely produce lasting transformation because students are left without continuous mentoring, structure, follow-up, and accountability. IGNITE addresses this by creating a continuous school-based system that follows students over time and helps them grow through repeated engagement.

## 3. The IGNITE Solution

IGNITE Leadership Club is a structured youth orientation and leadership development system for secondary schools. It uses the school environment as the main platform for student transformation, while involving teachers, counsellors, NYSC SDGs CDS members, guest speakers, school liaison officers, and the central IGNITE team.

The programme helps students develop:

- Improved behaviour and personal discipline
- Leadership skills and confidence
- Career clarity and future direction
- Civic and social responsibility
- Awareness of the Sustainable Development Goals
- Practical community service experience
- Communication, teamwork, and problem-solving skills

The central idea is simple:

**IGNITE is not a seminar. It is a youth development system inside schools.**

## 4. Vision and Mission

### Vision

To raise a generation of Nigerian youths who are prepared to lead, serve, think responsibly, and contribute positively to society.

### Mission

To build a sustainable school-based leadership and orientation system that equips secondary school students with the values, skills, confidence, civic awareness, and career direction needed for personal growth and community transformation.

## 5. Strategic Positioning

IGNITE should be positioned to government and sponsors as a low-cost, school-based youth orientation and leadership development system that supports education reform, youth development, civic responsibility, security prevention, human capital development, and community resilience.

For government, IGNITE supports state education and youth-development priorities by strengthening student behaviour, leadership, counselling, citizenship, and social stability.

For NGOs and development partners, IGNITE provides a structured youth empowerment model focused on SDGs, civic participation, life skills, and community impact.

For CSR sponsors and private philanthropists, IGNITE provides a measurable social investment opportunity with clear student outcomes, community visibility, and long-term impact potential.

## 6. Evidence of Early Demand

IGNITE already has early validation that the programme is needed and welcome in schools.

Two secondary schools have agreed to participate as pilot schools. A proposal has also been submitted through the Assistant to the Commissioner of Education in Zamfara State. In addition, discussions have been held with about five teachers from different schools, and they expressed interest in the programme, noting that it would help students.

This early demand shows that IGNITE is not only based on personal observation. It has initial school-level acceptance, teacher validation, and access to government education channels.

## 7. Target Beneficiaries

The primary beneficiaries are secondary school students from JSS3 to SS3. This group is important because they are old enough to understand leadership, responsibility, career choices, and social issues, but still young enough for structured guidance to influence their mindset and behaviour before they fully transition into adulthood.

The pilot will involve 60 students:

- 2 pilot schools
- 30 students per school
- Students selected from JSS3 to SS3

A balanced student spread may include:

- JSS3: 5 students per school
- SS1: 8 students per school
- SS2: 9 students per school
- SS3: 8 students per school

This mix allows the programme to reach both junior and senior students while creating continuity within the school.

## 8. Pilot Objectives

The one-term pilot will focus on four measurable outcomes:

1. Improved student behaviour
2. Leadership skills
3. Career clarity
4. Community service preparation

The pilot promise can be stated as:

**Within one school term, IGNITE will help participating students demonstrate improved behaviour, stronger leadership confidence, clearer career direction, and practical community responsibility through structured training and student-led service planning.**

## 9. Key Impact Areas and Measurement

### Improved Student Behaviour

This will be measured through teacher and counsellor observations, attendance, punctuality, participation, discipline records, peer interaction, and visible changes in responsibility and attitude.

### Leadership Skills

This will be measured through student confidence, communication, teamwork, club roles, public speaking, decision-making, responsibility-taking, and project coordination.

### Career Clarity

This will be measured by students identifying career interests, relevant subjects, future pathways, mentors, scholarship opportunities, vocational options, and academic requirements.

### Community Service Projects

During the pilot, students will identify community problems and design possible service projects. Full project grants may be introduced during implementation or expansion after students have completed the orientation and leadership training phase.

## 10. Programme Delivery Model

IGNITE will use a collaborative delivery model involving:

- The central IGNITE team
- School liaison officers
- School counsellors and selected teachers
- NYSC SDGs CDS group
- Guest speakers and mentors
- Student executives

### Central IGNITE Team

The central IGNITE team will provide programme leadership, curriculum structure, partnerships, training coordination, monitoring, sponsor reporting, documentation, and expansion management.

### School Liaison Officers

School liaison officers will coordinate implementation within each school. They will manage communication with school leadership, support attendance tracking, coordinate sessions, work with teachers and counsellors, and send progress reports to the central IGNITE team.

### School Counsellors and Teachers

Teachers and counsellors will provide continuity within the school system. They will support student monitoring, behaviour tracking, mentorship, and guidance. IGNITE should also help make the counsellor role more active and effective inside schools.

### NYSC SDGs CDS Group

IGNITE will collaborate with the NYSC SDGs Community Development Service group. The group will support training on the Sustainable Development Goals, community responsibility, and sustainable development awareness.

NYSC members should be treated as a valuable partner, not the permanent backbone of the programme, because NYSC postings change. The permanent structure should remain the IGNITE system, school liaison officers, teachers, counsellors, and student leadership.

### Guest Speakers and Mentors

Guest speakers will expose students to leadership, career development, entrepreneurship, civic responsibility, and life experiences. They will add inspiration, credibility, and practical exposure.

### Student Executives

Student executives will help lead peer engagement, organize club activities, support attendance, encourage participation, and assist in planning student-led service activities.

## 11. One-Term Pilot Structure

The pilot will run for one school term. A recommended structure is:

### Weeks 1-2: Setup and Baseline

- Confirm participating schools
- Select 30 students per school
- Brief principals, teachers, counsellors, and liaison officers
- Conduct baseline assessment
- Introduce students to IGNITE values and expectations

### Weeks 3-5: Self-Leadership and Behaviour

- Discipline and responsibility
- Values and character
- Communication and confidence
- Peer influence and decision-making
- Personal goals and accountability

### Weeks 6-7: SDG and Civic Orientation

- Introduction to the Sustainable Development Goals
- Local community challenges
- Civic duty and social responsibility
- Role of young people in community development

### Weeks 8-9: Career Clarity and Mentorship

- Career interests and subject alignment
- Academic and vocational pathways
- Mentorship sessions
- Guest speaker exposure
- Scholarship and future opportunities

### Weeks 10-11: Community Service Planning

- Identify community or school problems
- Form student teams
- Develop simple project ideas
- Prepare project presentations
- Review project feasibility

### Week 12: Review and Reporting

- Student presentations
- Endline assessment
- Teacher/counsellor feedback
- Certificates and recognition
- Pilot documentation
- Impact report for sponsors and government

## 12. Sustainability Strategy

Sustainability is the foundation of IGNITE. The programme is designed to avoid the common weakness of one-time youth seminars. Instead of brief inspiration without follow-up, IGNITE creates a continuous structure within schools.

Sustainability will come through:

- School-based clubs
- Central IGNITE coordination
- School liaison officers
- Teacher and counsellor involvement
- Student executives
- Regular sessions
- Monitoring and evaluation
- Sponsor reporting
- Government adoption
- Scalable curriculum and training materials

This sustainability argument should be central in every sponsor and government conversation:

**A one-time seminar can inspire students for a few days, but IGNITE creates a system for continuous guidance, monitoring, mentorship, and measurable transformation.**

## 13. Sponsorship and Partnership Strategy

IGNITE will not place financial cost on schools. Schools are implementation partners, not payers. This allows schools to fully participate without financial pressure.

The main sponsor targets are:

- Zamfara State Government
- Local politicians and public officials
- Banks
- Telecom companies
- NGOs
- International development organizations
- Private philanthropists
- Companies with CSR interest in Zamfara and Northwest Nigeria

### Government Sponsorship Message

IGNITE supports education reform, youth orientation, student behaviour, civic responsibility, school counselling, human capital development, and long-term social stability.

### CSR Sponsorship Message

IGNITE gives companies a measurable way to invest in youth development, community impact, education, leadership, and social responsibility.

### NGO and Development Partner Message

IGNITE is a structured school-based prevention and empowerment model focused on youth agency, SDG awareness, civic participation, career direction, and community resilience.

## 14. Sponsorship Ask

The first sponsorship ask should be focused on three priority areas:

1. Cash funding for logistics and operations
2. Official endorsement
3. Training materials

### Cash Funding

Cash funding will support coordination, school visits, facilitator movement, monitoring, documentation, communication, administration, and implementation logistics.

### Official Endorsement

Government endorsement will give IGNITE legitimacy, improve access to schools, and show that the programme aligns with state education and youth-development priorities.

### Training Materials

Training materials will include student workbooks, facilitator guides, SDG learning materials, leadership worksheets, career worksheets, attendance tools, assessment forms, certificates, and reporting templates.

### Student Project Grants

Student project grants should be introduced in the second stage. After students complete the training phase and begin full implementation, selected student groups can receive small grants to carry out community service projects.

## 17. Government Adoption Pathway

The government adoption pathway should be simple:

1. Approve and endorse the two-school pilot
2. Support pilot implementation for one school term
3. Review the pilot impact report
4. Expand to additional schools in Zamfara State
5. Develop a state-wide school leadership and orientation model

The government should not be asked only for money. It should also be asked for legitimacy, access, endorsement, school coordination, and long-term adoption.

## 18. Scale-Up Strategy

After the pilot, IGNITE can scale in phases:

### Phase 1: Two-School Pilot

Run the one-term pilot, collect data, produce an impact report, and document lessons.

### Phase 2: Zamfara Expansion

Expand to more schools across selected local government areas, using trained liaison officers and school coordinators.

### Phase 3: State Adoption

Work with the Ministry of Education and relevant agencies to adopt IGNITE as a structured youth leadership and orientation programme across secondary schools.

### Phase 4: Northwest Nigeria Expansion

Use the Zamfara evidence base to engage other Northwest states, NGOs, development partners, CSR sponsors, and private philanthropists.

## 19. Key Risks and Mitigation

### Risk: Programme depends too much on external facilitators

Mitigation: Build the programme around IGNITE structure, school liaison officers, teachers, counsellors, and student executives, while using NYSC members and guest speakers as supporting partners.

### Risk: Schools show interest but do not maintain participation

Mitigation: Secure principal approval, appoint school liaison officers, involve teachers/counsellors, track attendance, and maintain regular communication.

### Risk: Sponsors see it as just another seminar

Mitigation: Emphasize that IGNITE is a school-based system with continuity, monitoring, evaluation, and end-of-term reporting.

### Risk: Impact is difficult to prove

Mitigation: Use baseline and endline assessments, teacher feedback, student reflections, attendance records, behaviour indicators, and project outputs.

### Risk: Government endorsement is delayed

Mitigation: Start with school-level pilot approval while continuing ministry engagement and building evidence that strengthens the government case.
